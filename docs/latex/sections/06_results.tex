\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Overview}

All 72 simulation runs completed successfully and produced valid metric outputs. Test-set AUC-ROC values ranged from 0.1554 (ResNet50 / AdaDelta / LR=1e-4, a degenerate collapse) to 0.9995 (VGG19 / Adam and VGG19 / Adamax, both at LR=1e-4). Ten runs were identified as degenerate: the model collapsed to predicting a single class, yielding either recall=0 or specificity=0 (Section~\ref{subsec:degenerate}). The remaining 62 runs produced meaningful discriminative performance.

\subsection{Learning Rate Analysis}

Learning rate had a pronounced effect on overall performance. At LR=1e-4, the 24 runs achieved a mean AUC of 0.8805, a maximum of 0.9995, and a minimum of 0.1554 (the latter being a degenerate collapse by ResNet50/AdaDelta). At LR=1e-5, mean AUC fell to 0.7539 with a maximum of 0.9933. At LR=1e-6, mean AUC collapsed further to 0.6003, with only VGG19/Adamax reaching a credible 0.8713. \subsection{Degenerate Runs}
\label{subsec:degenerate}

Ten runs collapsed to predicting a single class throughout the test set, producing either recall=0 (predicting all non-cancer) or specificity=0 (predicting all cancer). These configurations failed to find a meaningful decision boundary. Table~\ref{tab:degenerate} lists all degenerate runs with their recall, specificity, and AUC values.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \caption{Degenerate runs (recall=0 or specificity=0) identified across 72 runs.}
  \label{tab:degenerate}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{X l l l r r r}
    \toprule
    \rowcolor{tableheader}
    \textbf{Architecture} & \textbf{Optimizer} & \textbf{LR} & \textbf{Failure Mode} & \textbf{Recall} & \textbf{Specificity} & \textbf{AUC} \\
    \midrule
    VGG19          & Adam     & 1e-6 & recall=0 & 0.0000 & 1.0000 & 0.5520 \\
    VGG19          & Adagrad  & 1e-4 & recall=0 & 0.0000 & 1.0000 & 0.6997 \\
    VGG19          & Adagrad  & 1e-6 & recall=0 & 0.0000 & 1.0000 & 0.6667 \\
    VGG19          & SGD      & 1e-6 & spec=0   & 0.9606 & 0.0000 & 0.3815 \\
%    VGG19          & Nadam    & 1e-6 & recall=0 & 0.0000 & 1.0000 & 0.8201 \\ % Nadam excluded
    EfficientNetB3 & Adamax   & 1e-6 & recall=0 & 0.0000 & 1.0000 & 0.8089 \\
    EfficientNetB3 & SGD      & 1e-6 & recall=0 & 0.0000 & 0.9083 & 0.1893 \\
    ResNet50       & Adagrad  & 1e-5 & recall=0 & 0.0000 & 1.0000 & 0.5074 \\
    ResNet50       & AdaDelta & 1e-4 & recall=0 & 0.0000 & 0.9917 & 0.1554 \\
    ResNet50       & AdaDelta & 1e-6 & spec=0   & 1.0000 & 0.0000 & 0.8350 \\
    ResNet50       & RMSProp  & 1e-6 & recall=0 & 0.0000 & 1.0000 & 0.7809 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Notably, some degenerate runs still produce moderate-to-high AUC values (e.g., EfficientNetB3/Adamax/1e-6: AUC=0.8089 despite recall=0). This occurs because AUC measures ranking ability across all thresholds, not performance at the specific 0.5 threshold used for binary prediction. A model that predicts all-negative with a systematic score ordering can achieve moderate AUC without ever correctly classifying a positive sample.
